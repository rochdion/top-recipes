Cake ananas

Recipe's description

The cake ananas is a simple cake with slices of pineapple on top and yet it is fatal. You will fall in love with it.

Each bite of it will bring you back to childhood time when you could eat sweets in front of your parents without fear.

Ingredients

  • 1 can of pineapple slices (reserve the juice),
  • 150 g flour
  • 100 g sugar
  • 100 g melted butter
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 packet of baking powder
  • 50 g sugar for the caramel

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. In a loaf pan or round cake pan, melt the 50g of sugar with a splash of water, either directly in the oven or in a saucepan, to create a light caramel, then coat the bottom of the pan with it.
  3. Arrange the drained pineapple slices over the caramel.
  4. In a mixing bowl, whisk together the eggs and sugar, then add the melted butter, followed by the flour and baking powder./li>
  5. Stir 3 to 4 tablespoons of the reserved pineapple juice into the batter.
  6. Pour the batter over the pineapple slices and bake for 40 minutes.
  7. Turn the cake out onto a serving platter while still warm.
